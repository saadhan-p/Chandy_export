Our Foundation

Bridging Artisanal Provenance With Global Commerce

Founded with a vision to institutionalize India's specialized agricultural and timber exports, Chandy's Global Exports operates as a premier B2B trading house.

Our operations span shade-grown coffee plantations in the mist-covered hills of Kodagu, specialized timber drying and quarter-sawing facilities in Karnataka, and dedicated export desks at Nhava Sheva (Mumbai) and Cochin ports.

We work directly with masterluthiers, international coffee roasters, and architectural interior firms who demand absolute consistency, certified moisture content, and transparent phytosanitary compliance.

Uncompromising Quality Controls

Laboratory Testing & Grading Standards

Every lot is inspected, acoustic-velocity tested, or cupping scored before commercial packing.

01. Ultrasonic Resonance

Acoustic Velocity Grading

All acoustic guitar tonewood billets undergo ultrasonic velocity measurement to guarantee longitudinal elasticity and stiffness parameters (>5,000 m/s).

02. SCA Cupping Protocol

Coffee Quality Assessment

Licensed Q-Graders evaluate every Kodagu green coffee harvest for moisture content (11%–12%), screen uniformity, and sensory cupping scores (84+ SCA certified).

03. ISPM-15 Treatment

Phytosanitary Certification

All export wooden crating and palleted shipments undergo certified heat treatment (HT 56°C for 30 min) in compliance with international ISPM-15 quarantine protocols.
